# P0171 - System Too Lean (Bank 1)

## System
Powertrain (Fuel / Air Intake)

## Description
The air/fuel ratio sensor (or oxygen sensor) on Bank 1 indicates the engine is running
leaner than the commanded value, meaning there is too much air or too little fuel in the
combustion mixture.

## Possible Causes
- Vacuum leak in intake hoses, gaskets, or PCV system
- Dirty or failing mass airflow (MAF) sensor
- Weak fuel pump or clogged fuel filter
- Clogged or underperforming fuel injectors
- Exhaust leak upstream of the oxygen sensor

## Diagnostic Steps
1. Inspect intake hoses and vacuum lines for cracks or disconnections.
2. Check MAF sensor readings against expected values at idle and 2500 RPM.
3. Measure fuel trim values (short-term and long-term) to confirm lean condition severity.
4. Test fuel pressure and volume against specification.
5. Inspect exhaust manifold and piping for leaks upstream of the oxygen sensor.

## Recommended Service Actions
- Repair or replace any vacuum leaks found.
- Clean or replace the MAF sensor if readings are out of range.
- Replace fuel filter and/or fuel pump if pressure/volume is insufficient.
- Address exhaust leaks near the oxygen sensor.

## Parts / Tools
Smoke machine, MAF sensor cleaner, fuel pressure gauge, OBD-II scan tool with live data.

## Safety Notes
A sustained lean condition can lead to engine overheating in individual cylinders and
accelerated component wear; recommend addressing within a reasonable service window.
