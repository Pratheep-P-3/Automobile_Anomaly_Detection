# P0505 - Idle Air Control System Malfunction

## System
Powertrain (Idle Control)

## Description
The PCM has detected an out-of-range condition in the idle air control (IAC) system,
resulting in idle speed that is unstable, too high, or too low compared to the target.

## Possible Causes
- Carbon buildup in the IAC valve or throttle body
- Faulty IAC valve
- Vacuum leak affecting idle air balance
- Dirty throttle body causing incorrect airflow at idle

## Diagnostic Steps
1. Inspect throttle body and IAC valve for carbon/varnish buildup.
2. Test IAC valve operation using bidirectional scan tool commands.
3. Check for vacuum leaks near the throttle body and intake manifold gasket.
4. Monitor idle RPM stability across multiple key cycles.

## Recommended Service Actions
- Clean throttle body and IAC valve passages of carbon deposits.
- Replace the IAC valve if it fails the bidirectional operation test.
- Repair any vacuum leaks contributing to idle instability.

## Parts / Tools
Throttle body cleaner, bidirectional OBD-II scan tool, smoke machine.

## Safety Notes
Stalling at stops can be a safety concern in traffic. If stalling is reported, recommend
scheduling service within a few days rather than waiting for routine maintenance.
