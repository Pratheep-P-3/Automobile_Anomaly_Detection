# P0301 - Cylinder 1 Misfire Detected

## System
Powertrain (Ignition / Fuel Delivery)

## Description
The PCM has detected a misfire isolated to cylinder 1. Unlike P0300, this code points to a
component or condition specific to a single cylinder rather than a system-wide issue.

## Possible Causes
- Faulty spark plug or ignition coil on cylinder 1
- Damaged or disconnected fuel injector wiring for cylinder 1
- Low compression in cylinder 1 (worn piston rings, valve, or head gasket leak)
- Carbon buildup on intake valve for cylinder 1

## Diagnostic Steps
1. Swap the ignition coil and spark plug from cylinder 1 with another cylinder and re-scan;
   if the misfire follows the swapped part, that part is faulty.
2. Check injector wiring harness and connector for cylinder 1.
3. Perform a compression test on cylinder 1 and compare to other cylinders.
4. Inspect intake valve for carbon deposits using a borescope if available.

## Recommended Service Actions
- Replace faulty spark plug or coil identified during the swap test.
- Repair or replace damaged injector wiring.
- If compression is low, further teardown inspection is required (valve or ring wear).
- Perform intake valve cleaning if carbon buildup is confirmed.

## Parts / Tools
Compression tester, borescope, spark plug socket set, multimeter.

## Safety Notes
Single-cylinder misfires are generally lower risk than multi-cylinder misfires but should
still be addressed promptly to avoid catalytic converter damage and fuel economy loss.
