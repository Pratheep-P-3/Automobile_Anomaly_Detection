# C0035 - Left Front Wheel Speed Sensor Circuit Malfunction

## System
Chassis (ABS / Traction Control)

## Description
The ABS module has detected an abnormal signal, open circuit, or short in the left front
wheel speed sensor circuit, disabling ABS and traction control functions as a safety
precaution.

## Possible Causes
- Damaged or corroded wheel speed sensor wiring/connector
- Faulty wheel speed sensor
- Damaged tone ring/reluctor ring on the wheel hub
- Excessive air gap between sensor and tone ring due to wear

## Diagnostic Steps
1. Visually inspect the sensor wiring harness for chafing, corrosion, or damage near the
   wheel well and suspension components.
2. Measure sensor resistance and compare to specification.
3. Inspect the tone ring for missing teeth or physical damage.
4. Check sensor air gap against manufacturer specification.

## Recommended Service Actions
- Repair or replace damaged wiring/connectors.
- Replace the wheel speed sensor if resistance is out of specification.
- Replace the tone ring or hub assembly if the ring is damaged.

## Parts / Tools
Multimeter, wheel speed sensor, feeler gauge for air gap, wiring repair kit.

## Safety Notes
This disables ABS and traction control, which are active safety systems. Recommend
scheduling service promptly (within 1-2 days) and advising the customer that braking
distances may increase on slippery surfaces until repaired.
